## Underneath

Built on [Prism](https://github.com/Vaspyyy/prism) 0.9.0, the library the tools share. Nothing else changed.

- **Changes land in the definition they are in.** recap took a definition to end at the first `{` or `;` after its name. So a change in the body of `fn f() -> [i32; 3] {` was put down to the `impl` around it, which happened to 1.5% of the functions in a large Rust project. The same went for `func f() interface{} {` in Go, `function f(): { ok: boolean } {` in TypeScript, definitions without a block of their own (`fun name() = id`, `const abs = (n) => …`) and Kotlin functions whose body is a single expression. They all end where they really end now.
- **Kotlin's `enum class`, `fun interface`, `inner class` and `operator fun` have their names.**
