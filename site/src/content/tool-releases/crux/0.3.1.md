## Fixed

- **A failed test's message keeps up to 15 lines instead of 6.** crux cut each failure in a Go, pytest or cargo run to 6 lines before the budget saw it. A message of several lines (a dumped output, a long diff) lost its end, even for the first failure, whose explanation the budget promises to keep. The budget still decides how much fits.
