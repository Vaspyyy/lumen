## Fixed

- **A pytest failure whose ID has a bracket that doesn't pair up keeps its message.** With `--tb=no` or `--tb=line`, crux reads failures from pytest's short summary. A parameter such as `test_bad[[]` never closed its bracket, so the message after it became part of the test ID: the failure had no message, its test wasn't found, and no rerun was suggested. The ID now ends at the first dash with a space on each side where its brackets pair up, and only where none does, at the first one that ends in `]`.
- **Parameters that hold a dash between spaces** (`test_eval[x[0] - y]`) and **folders with brackets in their names** (`[id]/test_x.py`) are read right too.

Nothing else changes.
