The first public release of glint. It is 0.5.0 with two fixes.

## Fixed

- **Dashboard rows don't wrap in a narrow terminal.** `glint -P` now drops columns until a row fits, the least useful first: the branch, then the 12 weeks of activity, then the stack. Only after that does it shorten project names. It keeps the last column free, since writing into it makes some terminals wrap, and the line of totals under the table is cut to the width too.
- **The disk map fills the window.** `glint -M` took a fixed share of the terminal's height. It now takes the whole height, less the title and the note on what could be freed, up to 60 lines instead of 40.
