## New

- **glint knows lumen.** lumen draws pictures sent with the kitty graphics protocol, and glint now counts it among the terminals that do, by the `TERM_PROGRAM=lumen` it sets. `glint --thumbs` shows real thumbnails there instead of half-block characters, as it already did in kitty, WezTerm and Ghostty. This went with the pictures of [lumen 0.2.0](/releases/0.2.0/); see [Pictures](/docs/images/).

An older glint, or one on another machine you are logged in to, can't tell that it runs in lumen. `glint --thumbs=kitty` tells it.
