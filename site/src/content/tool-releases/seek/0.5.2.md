seek's command line now follows the rest of the family. This came out on the same day as [lumen 0.5.2](/releases/0.5.2/), which made the same change.

## Changed

- **`-V` prints the version**, as `--version` does. It is the short flag for the version in glint, crux, recap, pulse and lumen too. `-v` is not a seek flag, so seek reports it as unknown instead of guessing.
- **An unknown flag points to the help**, as the other tools say it: `unknown flag --bogus (see seek --help)`.
- **`seek --help` begins with `✦ seek` and the version**, like the help of recap and crux. It is in sections (the pattern, what to show, matching, where to search, output, context, files) and ends with a few examples. The headings are colored on a terminal, unless `NO_COLOR` is set.
