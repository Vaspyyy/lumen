## Fixed

- **A theme written for Alacritty pastes in as it is.** The themes people share carry tables for parts of Alacritty that lumen doesn't have (`search`, `hints`, `vi_mode_cursor`, `footer_bar`, `line_indicator`, `dim`, `indexed_colors`), and say `CellForeground` where a color could be. lumen took those for mistakes in the file. It accepts them now and leaves them aside. A typo in a name is still reported. See [Colors](/docs/colors/).
- **`kitten icat` shows its pictures.** It waited forever instead: it asks the terminal what kind of terminal it is, and lumen's answer was too short for it to accept. See [Pictures](/docs/images/).
