lumen leaves the desk. This version brings an app for Android, and with it a new kind of terminal: one the PC keeps running, whether a window shows it or not.

## New

- **An app for Android.** It opens terminals that run on your PC, with your shell and your tools. Lock the phone, and they run on. Tabs, a row of the keys a phone keyboard lacks, pinch to change the size of the text, select and copy with a finger. See [lumen on your phone](/docs/phone/).

- **Pairing with a code.** `lumen pair` on the PC shows a code, the app scans it, the PC asks whether to let the phone in. No account and no server in between: the phone logs in over SSH, to the SSH server your PC already runs.

  ```sh
  lumen pair
  ```

- **A key that stays in the phone.** The phone's key is made in its secure hardware and can't be copied out of it. It works only after you unlock the phone with your fingerprint or screen lock. `lumen phones` lists the paired phones, `lumen unpair` takes one out. [How the phone logs in](/docs/phone-security/) says what is stored where.

- **Terminals that keep running.** `lumen attach --session work` shows a terminal the PC keeps. Close the window or lose the connection, and it runs on. Say the command again, anywhere, and it is back with what was on its screen. This is what the app opens, and it works without a phone too: over SSH from another machine, or to carry a terminal from one window to another. See [Terminals that keep running](/docs/kept-terminals/).

  ```sh
  lumen attach --session work
  lumen sessions
  ```

## Good to know

- The app is a file in the release, `lumen-android.apk`, not an app in the Play Store. [Install](/docs/install/#the-android-app) says how to get it and how to check it.
- It needs Android 11 or newer and a screen lock, and lumen 0.3.0 on the PC.
- It is a first version. Pictures, splits and search are not in it yet, and it uses lumen's own font and colors.
