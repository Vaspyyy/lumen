## Fixed

- **Block characters join.** The code that `lumen pair` shows had thin lines running through it, and so had every bar, chart and picture that a program builds from block characters such as `▀`, `▄` and `█`. lumen took them from the font, whose shapes don't fill a cell exactly and are soft at the edges. It fills them itself now, on the cell's own edges, so that cells next to each other join. The shades `░`, `▒` and `▓` are an even tone instead of the font's dots. See [Fonts](/docs/fonts/#characters-lumen-draws-itself).
- **Backgrounds, the selection and the cursor end where their cells end.** They reached up to a pixel into the cell beside them.

The Android app is the one from 0.3.0 with a new version number.
