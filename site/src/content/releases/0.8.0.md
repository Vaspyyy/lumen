lumen 0.7 put the settings in a menu. This release adds about thirty more, all about how lumen looks, and a new page for them: [Looks](/docs/looks/).

## A see-through window, as see-through as you set it

lumen's own frame casts a shadow, and the shadow was drawn under the whole window, not only around it. Through a see-through window (`opacity` below 1) it showed as a dark veil, so the window looked darker than `opacity` says, and brighter with `decorations = "none"`, which has no shadow. Now the shadow lies around the window only, as on a web page. It is as dark and as wide as before, and it costs nothing more to draw.

## The window

- **The frame**: how round the corners are (`corner_radius`, 0 to 40), and the border and the shadow each on or off. Everything inside keeps to the round corners, the tab bar and a picture too.
- **A picture behind the text**: `background_image`, how much of it shows, and whether it covers the window, fits in it whole, or is stretched to it. PNG, JPEG, WebP, SVG, and a GIF that moves keeps moving. On a see-through window, the picture is part of the glass.
- **A gradient**: the background fades into a second color, from the top down or at any angle, and can lie under a picture.

## The tab bar, the panes and the cursor

- **The tab bar at the bottom**: `position = "bottom"` in `[tabs]`.
- **Panes**: how far the panes you aren't typing in step back, or not at all, and how thick the line between two panes is.
- **The cursor**: how bright its glow is and how far it reaches, how long it takes to glide, how dim it gets when it blinks (0 goes out, as in other terminals) and how fast it fades.
- **Letters**: space between characters, and the font and size of lumen's own words in the tabs, the notes and the search.

## lumen's own colors

The new `[colors.ui]` table colors lumen's own words, the border, the lines between panes, the tab bar, the tab you are in and the shadow. Each can be see-through, as `#rrggbbaa`. Left unset, lumen's words are white on a dark background and black on a light one, where before they were always white, so a light theme works without setting anything.

## In the menu

Most of the new settings are in the settings menu (`/settings`): the corners, the shadow and the border, a picture with its strength and fit, the tab bar's place, the panes, the glow, the glide, the blink, the letter spacing and the size of lumen's words. A picture is typed in from `/` or `~/`, and lumen checks that it is there and is a picture it can show.

[Looks](/docs/looks/) has every setting, and four looks to start from: glass, a picture, nothing but text, and neon. Without a config file nothing changes: every default is how lumen looked before.

## Fixed

- **Dragging the line between two panes** follows the mouse exactly. It was a pixel off, and further with wider lines.

The Android app is the one from 0.3.0 with a new version number.
